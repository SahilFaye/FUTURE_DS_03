## 📌 Project Overview
This project evaluates a multi-channel digital marketing funnel to analyze customer drop-off dynamics, traffic-to-lead conversions, channel acquisition efficiency, and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC). The objective is to identify friction points across the funnel and formulate data-backed optimization strategies.


## 📊 Key Funnel Metrics
* *Total Visitors:* 81,000+
* *Total Leads:* 14,000+
* *Total Customers:* 3,000+
* *Overall Lead Conversion Rate:* 19.65%
* *Average CAC:* $16.43


## 🔍 Key Insights & Strategic Findings
1. *High-Intent Inbound Performance:* Email Marketing and Organic Search demonstrate the highest conversion efficiency with remarkably low acquisition costs, delivering strong overall revenue contribution.
2. *Paid Channel Drop-off Dynamics:* Meta Ads deliver high top-of-funnel impression volume but suffer significant attrition during the SQL qualification stage, resulting in higher marginal customer acquisition costs.
3. *High-Value B2B Conversions:* LinkedIn Ads yield steady, qualified lead conversion volumes with optimal commercial revenue returns despite a higher cost per click.
